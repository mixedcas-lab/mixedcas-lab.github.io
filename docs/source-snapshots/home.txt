Mixed-signal Circuits and Systems Laboratory
혼성신호 회로 및 시스템 연구실
We design mixed-signal integrated circuits and systems, from high-speed data converters for wireline and wireless links to low-noise readout for biomedical, image-sensor and memory applications.
